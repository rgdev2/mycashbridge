Best Personal Loan in India (2026): Rates, Eligibility & How to Choose

MyCashBridge.com Editorial– 7 min read–Updated June 2026

A personal loan is one of the fastest ways to fund a wedding, medical emergency, travel or any urgent need – without pledging collateral. But with dozens of banks and NBFCs offering different rates, choosing the best personal loan in India can feel confusing. This guide breaks it down in plain language.

What makes a personal loan the 'best' for you

The cheapest advertised rate is not always the best deal. The right loan balances the interest rate, processing fee, tenure flexibility and how fast you get the money. Focus on the all-in cost, not just the headline rate.

  • Interest rate – currently from around 10.5% p.a. for strong profiles
  • Processing fee – usually 1–3% of the loan amount
  • Tenure – 12 to 72 months; longer means smaller EMIs but more total interest
  • Disbursal speed – the best lenders fund within 24 hours
  • Prepayment terms – look for low or nil foreclosure charges

Eligibility for a personal loan in India

While each lender sets its own rules, most look for:

  • Age between 21 and 60 years
  • A steady monthly income (often ₹15,000+ depending on city)
  • A credit score of 700+ for the best rates
  • Stable employment or business history

Documents you'll typically need

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Latest 3 months' bank statements
  • Salary slips (salaried) or ITR (self-employed)
  • A passport-size photograph and address proof

How to get the lowest rate

  • Keep your credit score healthy (pay EMIs and card bills on time)
  • Reduce existing obligations before applying
  • Compare offers first, then apply once to avoid multiple hard enquiries
  • Choose a tenure you can comfortably afford
