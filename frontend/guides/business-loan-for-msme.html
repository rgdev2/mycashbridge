Business Loan for MSMEs in India (2026): Eligibility, Documents & Tips

MyCashBridge.com Editorial· 7 min read·Updated June 2026

Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) are the backbone of India's economy — but cash flow gaps and growth plans often need outside funding. A business loan can help you buy stock, hire, upgrade equipment or expand. Here's how MSME loans work.

Types of MSME finance

  • Working capital loans — smooth out day-to-day cash flow
  • Term loans — fund equipment, expansion or new premises
  • Collateral-free loans — unsecured funding up to a limit based on your turnover and credit
  • Loan against property — larger amounts at lower rates by pledging property

Eligibility for an MSME loan

  • Business vintage of typically 1–2 years
  • Minimum annual turnover as per lender norms
  • GST registration and a business bank account
  • Acceptable credit history of the business and promoters

Documents you'll usually need

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor/partners
  • Business registration / GST certificate
  • Last 6–12 months' bank statements
  • Last 1–2 years' ITR and financials

Tips to get approved faster

  • Keep your GST filings and bank statements clean and up to date
  • Maintain a healthy current account balance
  • Separate personal and business finances
  • Keep your and your business's credit healthy
