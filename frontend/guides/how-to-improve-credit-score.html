How to Improve Your Credit Score in India: A Simple 2026 Guide

MyCashBridge.com Editorial– 6 min read–Updated June 2026

Your credit score is the single biggest factor in whether you get a loan and at what rate. The good news: improving it is straightforward once you understand what drives it. Here's a clear, no-jargon guide.

What affects your CIBIL score

  • Payment history (~35%) – paying EMIs and bills on time is the biggest factor
  • Credit utilisation (~30%) – how much of your credit limit you use
  • Credit age & mix – older accounts and a healthy mix of loans/cards help
  • Hard enquiries – too many applications in a short time lower your score

Habits that lift your score

  • Never miss a due date – set auto-pay or reminders
  • Keep credit-card spends under 30% of your limit
  • Don't close your oldest credit card without reason
  • Apply for new credit sparingly and only when needed
  • Check your report yearly and dispute any errors

How long does it take?

There's no overnight fix. With consistent on-time payments and lower utilisation, most people see meaningful improvement within 3 to 6 months, and strong results over a year.

A better score saves you real money

Even a 1% lower interest rate on a large loan can save you tens of thousands of rupees over the tenure. Improving your score is one of the highest-return financial habits you can build.
