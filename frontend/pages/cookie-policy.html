This Cookie Policy explains how MyCashBridge uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

The website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyse traffic and user behaviour
  • Support advertising and remarketing campaigns
  • Enhance customer experience

Managing your cookies

When you first visit, we ask for your choice. Essential cookies are always active because the site needs them to function. Analytics and marketing cookies only run if you click “Accept all” or enable them in cookie settings. If you reject non-essential cookies, we do not set or keep them — and any that try to set are cleared automatically.

You can change your choice anytime by clicking “Cookie settings” in the website footer. You may also disable cookies through your browser settings; however, certain website features may not function properly.
